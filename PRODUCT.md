# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Rootstock serves macOS security analysts and penetration testers who investigate
attack paths, blue-team and compliance operators who repeat audits and produce
remediation evidence, security researchers who need transparent graph and Cypher
details, and report recipients who need findings they can read and print.

Operators are usually expert or domain-competent, work episodically for focused
30–90 minute investigations, and need to distinguish observed, inferred, stale,
empty, and failed states without ambiguity.

## Product Purpose

Rootstock is a passive, local-first macOS attack-path analysis workstation for
authorized security work. It turns local scan evidence into an inspectable graph,
supports filter–inspect–path–query workflows, allows reversible local analysis
annotations, and produces trustworthy security reports. Success means an analyst
can connect, establish provenance, find and explain a path, run or author a query,
and hand off a readable report without exposing scan data or tokens.

## Brand Personality

Precise, restrained, accountable. The interface should feel familiar to an
analyst who uses Wireshark, macOS Console, and Neo4j Browser: compact and direct,
with technical detail available instead of hidden.

## Anti-references

Rootstock is not a marketing dashboard, consumer security score, ornamental SOC
wallboard, or simplified no-code graph toy. Avoid decorative metrics, glow-heavy
dark UI, glass surfaces, gradient branding, colored card stripes, tiny uppercase
scaffolding, ornamental motion, and euphemistic language that obscures Cypher or
evidence provenance.

## Design Principles

1. Put evidence, provenance, and current system state before decoration.
2. Keep expert workflows fast while making every failure and reversible action explicit.
3. Treat Canvas as a visual enhancement and provide equivalent semantic operations.
4. Preserve local-first, offline-capable behavior and inspectable technical detail.
5. Use one consistent, dense component vocabulary across the workbench and reports.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support complete keyboard operation, visible and unobscured
focus, VoiceOver-equivalent graph exploration, 320 CSS-pixel reflow around the
two-dimensional graph exception, reduced motion, 44px targets in narrow/touch
layouts, and meaning that is never communicated by color alone. Reports must be
semantic, responsive, and printable.
