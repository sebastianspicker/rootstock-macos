# Non-goals

Rootstock Blue focuses on offline parsing, case management, detections, and
reporting. The following products and capabilities are outside that scope:

- a multi-platform endpoint monitor or enterprise EDR replacement;
- a SIEM, MDM service, or Santa decision engine;
- System Integrity Protection, TCC, or Full Disk Access bypasses;
- FileVault bypass or recovery without operator-supplied access material;
- Secure Enclave secret extraction or full physical-memory acquisition;
- full packet capture or a Packet Tunnel inspection product;
- a cloud detonation service;
- complete coverage of every mac_apt plugin or macOS artifact;
- export of Keychain secrets, private keys, session tokens, or `kcpassword`
  values.

For details about evidence access, parser coverage, and case durability, see
[platform limitations](limitations.md).
